Program tiles: original vs new

The same little board three times, no gameplay. A row of conveyor, step, clockwise rotator, conveyor, counter-clockwise rotator and conveyor, with a short vertical belt crossing it.
Original (2012): the old pre-drawn frames from images/sprytszyt.png at the old speed. That is 20 or 60 frames, each shown for 0.025 s (about 40 fps), stepped the way Quintus does it.
New v1 (current build): the frames now in the game, from images/tiles/program-tiles.png. That is 25 or 50 frames, each shown for 0.04 s (25 fps).
New, smooth (candidate): the same Toybox design drawn live on every screen refresh with anti-aliased vector shapes. The arrows are rotated in code, and the belt offset comes from the clock, so the motion is as smooth as your display allows. Speeds match the original.
At 1×, tiles are 45 px with 2 px gaps, as on the game board. The game canvas is not Retina-aware, so on a Retina screen “Canvas: game” is what the game shows. “Canvas: sharp” renders at device pixels.
