Style (exploration concept A, "Phosphor"): vector CRT phosphor display art, like an old oscilloscope or vector arcade monitor. Thin bright glowing green lines (#3dff7a) drawn over near-black green-tinted surfaces (#03110a), outlines only with little or no fill, one hue in two or three brightnesses, a faint soft bloom halo around every line. Hazards may glow amber instead of green. Crisp, minimal, geometric.
