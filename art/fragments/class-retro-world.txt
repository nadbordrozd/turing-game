Style (retro hacker chic skin): a real place outdoors at night as an old green-screen computer would draw it. Only near-black and phosphor green (bright #33ff66, plus one dim and one dark green step), no other hues. Crisp pixel art: dark silhouettes and flat dark-green shapes with thin 1-2 pixel glowing green edges and sparse lit dots, a faint glow, calm and uncluttered. Backdrop: one wide real landscape seen from a standing person's eye height, like a flat 2D game background, nobody in it, mostly dark and low in contrast so bright, colourful hand-drawn characters placed on top later stand out.
